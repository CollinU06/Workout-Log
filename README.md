# Workout Log

Log runs, lifts, walks, practices, etc. all in one place. The app works out the stats for you.

**Audience**

People who train in more than one way. Runners who also lift, athletes logging practice hours, anyone who bikes and walks and wants it all in one place. 

**What it does**

Add a workout, pick the type, enter what you did. Runs, bikes, and walks take distance and time and show your pace. Lifts take weight and reps and show total volume and an estimated 1 rep max. Practices and general workouts just take a duration to keep logs of.

Tap an entry to expand it and see the full breakdown. The add form changes its fields depending on the type you pick, so you're never entering distance for a bench press.

**Why it's useful**

3 miles in 30 minutes is a 10-minute mile. 135 pounds for 5 reps is 675 pounds of volume. Those numbers are more useful than the raw entry, and doing that math yourself after every session gets old.
